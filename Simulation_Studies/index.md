# Simulation studies

Two studies evaluated different aspects of *TransMap*. The [local-program study](local_program.md) tested whether aggregating weak effects across a co-expression module improved detection of a *trans*-QTL. The [global-program study](global_program.md) tested whether a multivariate method could recover variant-gene associations distributed through a directed regulatory network.

These pages specify the data-generating models, parameter values, comparison methods, and evaluation units used for the Figure 1 results. The notebooks in this repository read plot-ready RDS summaries; the computationally intensive simulation runs and fitted models are not distributed here. The [Figure 1c MOCK layout preview](../Main_Figures/Figure_1/Figure_1c_MOCK.ipynb) contains synthetic values and is not evidence for power or type-I error.
